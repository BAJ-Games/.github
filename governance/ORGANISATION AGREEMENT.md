# BAJ-Games Organization Agreement

Version 1.0
Effective Date: November 26, 2026

## 1. Purpose

This agreement establishes rules for the
management, administration and protection
of the BAJ-Games GitHub organization.

Its purpose is to ensure fair collaboration,
protect all contributors and prevent
unauthorized claims of ownership or control.

Both founders are considered equal
administrative partners unless otherwise
agreed in writing.

## 3. Organization Integrity

Neither founder may unilaterally:

- Claim exclusive ownership of the organization.
- Transfer ownership or administrative control.
- Remove the other founder's administrative
  privileges.
- Change the organization's fundamental
  administrative structure.
- Exclude the other founder from the organization.

Any such action requires the prior written
consent of both founders.

## 4. Administrative Rights

Both founders must retain appropriate
administrative privileges.

Neither founder may use administrative
privileges to unfairly disadvantage the other.

In exceptional circumstances involving
a demonstrable security threat, temporary
protective measures may be taken.

Such measures must be proportionate,
documented and communicated immediately.

## 5. Repository Protection

Repositories belonging to BAJ-Games
must be protected against unauthorized:

- Deletion.
- Transfer.
- Public release.
- Changes of ownership or control.
- Removal of contributors' access.

Major administrative changes require
the approval of both founders.

## 6. Intellectual Property

This agreement does not automatically
transfer intellectual property rights.

Each contributor retains the rights
to their own original contributions,
subject to applicable licenses and law.

Jointly created works remain subject
to applicable copyright law.

No founder may claim exclusive authorship
or copyright over another person's work
without a valid legal basis.

## 7. Historical Projects

Projects created before the establishment
of the BAJ-Games organization retain
their existing copyright and licensing status.

Moving a repository into BAJ-Games
does not automatically transfer ownership
or change existing licenses.

Historical contributions must be respected
according to their respective authorship
and applicable licensing terms.

No founder may unilaterally change
the licensing status of another person's
contributions.

## 8. Changes to This Agreement

Changes to this agreement require
the written approval of both founders.

No unilateral amendment is permitted.

## 9. Dispute Resolution

Both founders agree to attempt to resolve
disputes through mutual discussion.

Neither founder may use administrative
control as leverage to obtain ownership
or intellectual property rights.

## 10. Governing Law

This agreement shall be governed by
the laws of the Federal Republic of Germany.

Mandatory statutory provisions remain
unaffected.
